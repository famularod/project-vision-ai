# Vitruvius Product Constitution

This compatibility filename is retained temporarily. The canonical product is
**Vitruvius Project Intelligence, powered by ECOS.**

## 1. Product mission

Vitruvius reduces the time and effort required for project managers to
understand projects, make informed decisions, and communicate progress. It
turns field updates, photos, schedules, risks, history, and stakeholder needs
into practical, reviewable project intelligence.

The software must remove unnecessary work. Every product decision should save
time, improve understanding, improve decisions or communication, reduce risk,
or increase confidence in project status.

## 2. Canonical architecture

Vitruvius is the application people use. ECOS is the intelligence system.
ECOS Core interprets evidence and prepares proposed conclusions, updates,
risks, and actions. ECOS Assurance independently verifies evidence support,
confidence, identity, authority, business rules, required approvals,
synchronization, and release quality.

ECOS Core must not approve its own work. Outputs that affect project truth,
stakeholders, risk, safety, schedule, or commitments remain proposed until the
applicable ECOS Assurance checks and human approval are complete.

## 3. Product behavior

ECOS is a system, not a human-style assistant or character. Interface language
describes analysis, evidence, confidence, review, recommendations, uncertainty,
and verification. It does not assign ECOS a personality, relationship, or human
authority.

Vitruvius should:

- make the next useful action clear;
- capture accurate field evidence with minimal friction;
- distinguish observation, interpretation, and recommendation;
- preserve missing information instead of inventing it;
- prepare communication without bypassing user review;
- remember confirmed context and corrections safely; and
- keep technical diagnostics out of normal workflows.

## 4. Core product principles

- Customers should never need their own Gemini, OpenAI, Google Cloud, or
  Supabase account, and they should never enter an API key or purchase separate
  processing credits. Vitruvius owns, secures, operates, and pays for its
  required service integrations as part of the Vitruvius product.
- Complete common field workflows in under 60 seconds when practical.
- Give every screen one clear purpose and one primary action.
- Never ask for information Vitruvius can determine reliably.
- Never expose admin or diagnostic tools in daily workflows.
- Never sacrifice usability for flexibility.
- Never make users repeat a decision when a trustworthy default exists.
- Never let new intelligence make an existing workflow harder.
- Never let automated output bypass required human review.

AI-supported recommendations inform judgment; people retain decision authority.
Simplicity, evidence, and trust matter more than feature count.

## 5. Product capabilities

### Capture

Capture preserves photos, notes, areas, issues, safety concerns, completed work,
and next steps with accurate source context.

### ECOS Analysis

ECOS Analysis connects updates, schedules, photos, risks, milestones, open
issues, and history to identify what changed, what needs attention, and which
actions should be reviewed.

### Communication

Vitruvius prepares accurate, audience-appropriate drafts while preserving user
review and evidence traceability.

### Automation

Automation prefills known context, surfaces overdue work, prepares drafts, and
keeps project signals current without silently changing project truth.

### ECOS Memory

ECOS Memory uses confirmed information, corrections, and repeated patterns to
improve future analysis without becoming unpredictable or overriding current
evidence.

## 6. Success measures

- Understand the current project state in under 5 seconds.
- Capture a useful update in under 60 seconds.
- Find a project in under 15 seconds.
- Prepare an executive report in under one minute.
- Reduce repeated manual entry and unnecessary decisions.
- Improve the clarity of risks, issues, overdue work, and next actions.
- Keep daily workflows free of admin and diagnostic clutter.
- Verify saves, synchronization, and release quality through ECOS Assurance.

## 7. Product culture

Every feature must answer whether it saves time, improves understanding,
improves decisions or communication, and will be useful in daily project work.
Vitruvius favors clarity over cleverness, usefulness over novelty, and trust
over automation for its own sake.

## 8. Final statement

Vitruvius is the product. ECOS is the intelligence system. ECOS Core reasons.
ECOS Assurance verifies.
